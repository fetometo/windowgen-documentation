Starting Up
===========

Requirements
------------

WindowGen 4 runs on **Blender 4.2 LTS and newer** and is tested up to Blender 5.3. It is installed as a Blender extension.

.. note::
    Using Blender 4.0 or 4.1? Stay on WindowGen 3 and its `documentation <https://windowgen-documentation.readthedocs.io/en/3.0/>`_.

How to install WindowGen add-on
-------------------------------

The quickest way is to drag the WindowGen zip archive from your file manager into the Blender window and confirm the installation. To install it from the Preferences instead, follow these steps:

1. Click on **Edit** in the top left corner and select **Preferences…**.

2. Open the **Get Extensions** section (1), click the arrow in the top right corner (2) and choose **Install from Disk…**.

    .. image:: images/01_starting_up_install1.png
        :width: 75%

3. Navigate to the folder where you saved the WindowGen zip archive, select it and press **Install from Disk**. Do not unpack the archive.

4. WindowGen is enabled automatically. You can find it in the **Add-ons** section, where its preferences are located as well.

    .. image:: images/01_starting_up_install2.png
        :width: 75%

5. Close the Preferences window. Press **N** in the 3D Viewport to open the side panel and select the **WindowGen** tab.

    .. image:: images/01_starting_up_install5.png
        :width: 75%

Updating from WindowGen 3
-------------------------

1. Install WindowGen 4 as described above. It replaces the previous version; there is no need to remove WindowGen 3 first.
2. Open your project and press **Update WindowGen in This File** in the *Add Window* panel. See :doc:`working_with_generator` for details.

Your windows keep all of their settings.
